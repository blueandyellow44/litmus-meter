LITMUS Live: five ways to show the shape of a speech

Mockups only, for choosing. Nothing here is wired to the meter yet.

Lines come from Max's rally test on 2026-10-07 and the scores shown for them are what the meter gave. The device tags, highlights and repetition counts are illustrative: the meter does not produce them yet, and building any of them means the scorer must quote the words that show each one, the same evidence rule the LITMUS site uses for its highlights.

Option 1 · live and after

Heat ribbon

One bar per line as he speaks, colored by band and taller when hotter; dashed outlines are lines with no claim. The shape shows builds, spikes and cool-downs at a glance. Afterwards the peaks carry their quote; hover or tap any bar for its line.

Option 2 · live

Loaded words lit up

The speaker's own loaded words get the site's warm highlight; checkable facts get the cool underline; words he attributes to someone else stay grey and unmarked (the quote rule). Hover a highlight for why it was marked.

 

Option 3 · live chips, tally after

Rhetorical devices and fallacies

Each line can carry up to two tags, shown only when the scorer quotes the words that show it. Afterwards, a tally; red bars are logical fallacies, grey bars are devices (devices are not wrong in themselves).

Option 4 · after

What he kept coming back to

Phrases repeated across the speech, with a tick at each moment it was said, so you see whether it is a refrain or a burst.
