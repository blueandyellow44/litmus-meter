LITMUS Live: the speech report, three ways

Built from Max's description: a graph of how hot the speech ran over time, and an analysis of the rhetorical moves the speaker makes, their persuasive style, and the fallacies they lean on.

Lines and their heat scores are from Max's rally test on 2026-10-07. The move and fallacy labels, the persuasive-style split and the timings are illustrative; the meter does not produce them yet. Building this means the scorer names a move or fallacy only when it quotes the words that show it, the evidence rule the LITMUS site uses. The persuasive-style split uses Aristotle's three appeals: ethos (trust in the speaker), pathos (emotion), logos (facts and reasoning).
